About Me 🌸

I am a young woman who is discovering the world of technology and programming. I enjoy learning new things, creating websites and challenging myself to become better every day.

My journey has taught me that where you start does not determine where you can go. I believe that with consistency, patience and continuous learning, I can achieve my dreams.

My Story 🌸

I was once just a refugee girl 👧🏽, raised by a single parent, who believed that I did not have what it takes to do something great—something that would make others proud. 💔

As I grew older, I began to realize that I needed to do something more for my future. ✨ I chose to learn tailoring 🧵, but later I discovered that there was so much more I could become.

One day, I noticed that my brother was learning programming. 💻 When I looked at his computer, everything seemed complicated. When he encouraged me to learn programming too, I thought he wasn't serious. 😂 But he kept encouraging me to believe in myself.

Because of our difficult financial situation, I couldn't even afford a computer. I had only my phone 📱, but I refused to let that stop me.

Then, by the grace of God 🙏🏽, I joined Code Blossom 🌸. Code Blossom and my brother became like my second pair of legs 🦋, helping me move closer to the dreams I once thought were impossible.

Today, I am beginning to see my future shining. ✨ I have started believing in myself and accepting that I am capable of doing great things too. 💪🏽❤️

From a girl who once believed she couldn't, to a woman learning that she can. 👧🏽➡️👩🏽✨
